Privacy Policy for QR & Barcode Reader Generator

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to QR Scanner ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our QR code and barcode scanning application ("App") collects, uses, and protects your data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

1. Camera Access

  • Purpose: To scan QR codes and barcodes
  • The App accesses your device's camera to capture images for scanning purposes
  • Camera images are processed in real-time and are NOT stored or transmitted to any server
  • You can revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings

2. Scan History

  • Purpose: To provide you with a history of scanned and generated QR codes/barcodes
  • Data collected: scanned content (text, URLs, contact information, etc.), scan timestamp, barcode format, generated QR code images and settings
  • All scan history is stored LOCALLY on your device using Room Database
  • You can delete individual items or clear all history at any time within the App

3. Gallery/Photo Access

  • Purpose: To scan QR codes/barcodes from images in your photo library
  • Only the specific images you select for scanning are accessed
  • Images are processed temporarily and NOT stored by the App
  • You can revoke storage permission at any time through your device settings

4. Device Information (via Firebase Analytics)

  • Purpose: For analytics and app improvement
  • Data collected: device model and manufacturer, Android OS version, app version, screen size and resolution, language preference, app usage statistics, crash reports and performance data
  • Stored anonymously on Firebase servers

5. Advertising Information (via Google AdMob)

  • Purpose: To display relevant advertisements
  • Data collected: Advertising ID (GAID), device information, ad interaction data (views, clicks), general location (country/region level only — not precise location)
  • Managed by Google AdMob according to their privacy policy
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise location: We do NOT collect your GPS location or precise location data
  • Personal identifiable information: We do NOT collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other PII
  • Contact list: We do NOT access your contacts
  • Files: We do NOT access files outside of images you explicitly select for scanning
  • Microphone: We do NOT access your microphone or record audio
  • Phone calls/SMS: We do NOT read or send SMS messages or make phone calls without your explicit action

How We Use Your Information

Primary Functionality

  • To scan and decode QR codes and barcodes
  • To generate QR codes and barcodes
  • To save your scan history locally for your convenience
  • To provide scan result actions (open URLs, copy text, etc.)

App Improvement

  • To understand how users interact with the App
  • To identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • To improve performance and user experience
  • To develop new features based on usage patterns

Advertising

  • To display relevant advertisements
  • To measure ad performance
  • To support the free version of the App

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All scan history is stored LOCALLY on your device
  • We use Room Database with encryption for sensitive data
  • Data is NOT synchronized to any cloud service
  • If you uninstall the App, all local data is permanently deleted

Security Measures

  • We implement industry-standard security practices
  • Camera and storage access requires explicit user permission
  • No data transmission to external servers (except Firebase Analytics and AdMob as disclosed)
  • Regular security audits and updates

Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

1. Google Firebase Analytics

Purpose: App analytics and crash reporting. Data collected: anonymous usage statistics, device information, crash reports.
Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2. Google AdMob

Purpose: Advertising. Data collected: Advertising ID, device information, ad interactions.
Privacy Policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. ML Kit (Google)

Purpose: Barcode scanning. ML Kit processes barcode images on-device; no data is sent to Google servers.
Privacy Policy: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Children's Privacy

Our App does NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The App is designed for general audiences and does not target children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete such information.

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

  • View all your scan history within the App
  • Delete individual scan items or clear all history
  • Export scan history to CSV format

Permissions

  • Revoke camera access by disabling camera permission in device settings (App will not function for scanning)
  • Revoke storage access by disabling storage permission in device settings (App cannot scan from gallery)

Advertising

  • Opt-out of personalized ads: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Or reset your Advertising ID at any time

Data Deletion

  • Uninstalling the App deletes all local data permanently
  • You can also delete history manually within the App at any time

Data Retention

  • Scan History: Retained locally on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App
  • Analytics Data: Retained by Firebase according to their retention policies (typically 14 months)
  • Ad Data: Retained by AdMob according to their retention policies

International Data Transfers

  • Analytics and Ad Data may be transferred to and stored on Google servers worldwide
  • Scan History is stored locally on your device; no international transfers
